Privacy Policy for Aura Leveling

Last updated: October 3, 2026

What this app is

Aura Leveling is a gamified fitness app: verified real-world workouts (camera-tracked exercises, voice-tracked "Vocal Surge" challenges, pedometer-tracked steps) power up a fighter character used in the app's game modes. It also includes multi-week training programs, diet guidance and a workout tracker. Aura Leveling is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition — see the app's Terms of Service for the full health and safety disclaimer.

Data this app accesses, and why

  • Camera: used locally to detect and count body movements during workouts (e.g. push-up and sit-up tracking), including the Aura Trial fitness test and the short "hold still… go" checks that confirm a live person is exercising. Camera frames are processed on-device for pose detection and are never saved or transmitted anywhere. (With Cloud Save on, the live checks send only numbers about a joint angle, never frames — see "Cloud Save" below.) A photo is only sent elsewhere if you explicitly take one for one of the AI photo features — see "AI photo features (Google Gemini)" below, the only case where that happens.

  • AI photo features (Google Gemini): three features let you take a photo and get an AI-generated result:

    • Aura Selfie (Bio-scanner) — rates your pose or outfit;
    • Aura Selfie Fight — rates a fighting pose you strike, to decide a fun pose battle;
    • Meal Scanner — estimates a meal's calories and nutrients.

    To do this, the photo you take is sent from your phone directly to Google's Gemini API for analysis; it is not analysed on-device. This only happens if you have entered your own Google Gemini API key in the app, have agreed to the in-app notice shown before the first photo is sent, and then explicitly use one of these features. Nothing is sent otherwise. Your Gemini API key is stored using your device's secure keystore, never in plaintext. Use of the Gemini API is subject to Google's own privacy policy, and Google may retain what it receives under its terms. In an Aura Selfie Fight against a friend's phone, only your numeric score is sent to the other phone — never your photo.

  • Microphone: used locally during the "Vocal Surge" mini-game to measure volume (decibels). Audio is not recorded to a file or transmitted anywhere — only the momentary volume level is read.

  • Activity Recognition / step sensors: used to count daily steps via the device pedometer, which feeds into the in-app fitness score and program step goals.

  • Health Connect (steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, active calories, total calories burned, distance, floors climbed, sleep, blood oxygen, breathing rate, workouts, weight, height, body fat, blood pressure, hydration): if you connect a supported watch/band, the app reads whichever of these metrics you allow from Android Health Connect (or Apple Health on iOS). You can allow some and not others. How they're used, all on your device:

    • Steps count toward your in-app power level.
    • Your resting heart rate, if available, is one part of the optional Aura Trial fitness score, which sets part of your power level.
    • Heart rate during a camera-tracked workout is compared with your resting heart rate to confirm the workout was real; if it didn't rise, those reps count as casual rather than verified.
    • Weight and height keep the body stats used for your programs and diet targets up to date.
    • Everything is shown back to you (today's readings, a daily readiness suggestion, and a 30-day daily summary of resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, blood oxygen and breathing rate). Manually typed entries in Health Connect are ignored for steps and heart rate. None of this data is used for any medical purpose, and it never leaves your device. The app does not write data back to Health Connect or Apple Health, and this data is never sold or used for advertising.

  • Body, diet and training information you enter: to personalise programs and scores you can optionally enter your age, sex, height, weight and goal weight; your diet type (for example vegetarian or vegan) and any food allergies; weigh-ins; the calories and protein of meals you log; and gym or home workouts you record in the Workout Tracker (the exercises, sets, reps and weights). All of this is stored on your device only, is never transmitted anywhere, and is never sold or used for advertising.

  • WHOOP (only when online features are enabled and you connect it): if you link a WHOOP account, you sign in on WHOOP's own page. The access it grants (recovery, cycles/strain, sleep and workouts) is held by the app's server, never on your phone, and used only to fetch your latest summary — recovery, strain, sleep performance, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, skin temperature, breathing rate and today's workouts — which is shown back to you and kept in the same on-device 30-day summary. Like the watch data, it never affects your power level and is never sold or used for advertising. Unlinking revokes the access and deletes the server's copy of it. Linking does not turn on Cloud Save. (WHOOP's direct link isn't available in the current version; WHOOP owners can share WHOOP data to Health Connect instead, which is read as described above.)

  • Google Health (only when online features are enabled and you link it): instead of Health Connect, you can link your Google Health account (Fitbit, Pixel Watch) directly. You sign in on Google's own page and choose what to share: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, and sleep. The access is held by the app's server, never on your phone, and used only to fetch today's summary — steps, distance, floors, calories, heart rate (latest, range, resting), heart rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate, last night's sleep, today's workouts and your latest weight — plus your heart-rate readings during an AI Arena session, to check that session. The summary is shown back to you and kept in the same on-device 30-day summary; the server does not store your health data, only the access. Like the watch data, it never affects your power level, and it is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Unlinking revokes the access with Google and deletes it from the server. Linking does not turn on Cloud Save.

  • Bluetooth, nearby devices and (on older Android) location: used only when you duel or raid with a friend whose phone is nearby, through Google's Nearby Connections. The two phones connect directly and exchange only what the game needs: your fighter name and look (colour and equipped aura cosmetic), power level, training levels that affect the fight, each game's score, and your moves during a live fight. Nothing goes through a server, nothing is stored after the session, and your location is never read or stored (older Android versions require the location permission for any Bluetooth search).

  • Notifications: used for local reminders — daily streak alerts and, only if you turn them on, habit reminders timed to the workday hours you set. All reminders are scheduled on your device, not sent from a server, and can be turned off in the app or in your phone's settings.

  • Photos and storage (Save to phone): if you tap Save, the image is saved to your phone's own gallery (in an "Aura Leveling" album). Nothing is uploaded. On Android 10 and older this needs the storage permission; newer versions don't.

  • Reporting an AI result: if you report an AI result (from the Bio-scanner, Aura Selfie Fight or Meal Scanner), the report (the result's text, your reason and any note you add) is kept on your device only. It's sent to the developer only if you choose "Email it", which opens your own email app.

  • Opening your music app: Hyper-Sprint can open a playlist link you saved in your music app. The link stays on your device; nothing about your listening is read by Aura Leveling.

What's stored, and where

  • On-device: your profile — workout stats, programs and progress, body, diet and allergy information, meal and workout logs, weigh-ins, reminder settings, in-app currency, equipped items and game progress — is stored on your device. The app is fully usable offline, and unless you turn on Cloud Save (below), that's the only place it is stored.

  • Cloud Save (optional, off until you turn it on): the cloud button on the Select Fighter screen explains what is sent and asks before anything is. When it's on:

    • Sign-in is anonymous: the app gets a random account ID from Google Firebase Authentication, tied to this phone. No email, password or real name is asked for. (Linking WHOOP or Google Health creates the same kind of anonymous account to hold that link, without turning on Cloud Save.)
    • Your fighters' game progress is saved to Aura Leveling's server so you can restore it on another phone: fighter names, levels and Power, exercise and step totals and personal bests, Aura Trial rep counts and rating, training programs and progress, missions, streaks, in-app currency, gear and cosmetics.
    • Live checks are issued and judged by the server. Before a camera exercise set, the app asks the server for a schedule of "hold still… go" checks (it sends the exercise and your fighter's ID). Afterwards it sends evidence so the server can confirm a live person did the set: the rep count, how long the set took, when each check was called and answered, and a short series of numbers for the angle of the joint the camera was tracking. This is not video and not images — only numbers. The server checks them and doesn't keep the angle numbers. It keeps a record of each passing set (exercise, rep count, date) and returns a signed receipt, which is saved with your game progress. Your True Power on the leaderboard is worked out by the server from those receipts, so it can be trusted. A set done without a connection still counts in your own game but gets no receipt, so it isn't counted on the online True Power board.
    • Your fighter name and Power appear on the global leaderboards, visible to other signed-in players, and a short friend code derived from your account ID lets rivals send you trades and wagers (who proposed what, and the result, are kept to settle them).
    • Never sent, even with Cloud Save on: age, sex, height, weight, goal weight and weigh-ins; heart rate and every other Health Connect, watch, WHOOP or Google Health reading (including the resting heart rate in Aura Trial results); water and sleep check-ins; diet type and allergies; meal-scan photos; the Workout Tracker log; reminder times; and your profile picture or selfie.

    The server is Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions) in Google Cloud's Mumbai, India region; Google processes the data on Aura Leveling's behalf under its Firebase terms. It's sent encrypted (HTTPS) and is never sold or used for advertising. It's kept until you delete it (see "Deleting your data").

  • In-app purchases: Star Crystal purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Your payment details go to Google, never to Aura Leveling; the app only receives a purchase ID, which it keeps on your device so a purchase is never credited twice. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment itself.

  • Photos: a profile picture you choose is stored locally only. A photo taken for an AI photo feature is sent to Google's Gemini API for analysis (see above); Aura Leveling itself never keeps a copy on any server. Images you choose to save go to your phone's gallery, and images you choose to share go to the app you pick in your phone's share menu.

What this app does NOT do

  • No advertising SDK and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is currently integrated. (If one is added later — e.g. Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics — this policy will be updated to disclose it before that release ships.)
  • No selling of any data, and no use of health, body, diet or allergy information for advertising.
  • Workout-tracking camera frames and microphone audio are never uploaded or shared with anyone; they're processed on-device only. The one exception is the optional AI photo features, which send a photo you explicitly take to Google's Gemini API — see "AI photo features (Google Gemini)" above for when and why.

Your choices

  • Camera, microphone, activity, notification, Bluetooth / nearby devices, storage and location permissions can be denied or revoked at any time in your device's app settings, and Health Connect access can be withdrawn in Health Connect itself; the relevant features simply won't work without them, but the rest of the app still functions.
  • Body, diet and allergy details are optional: programs and the workout score work with sensible defaults if you leave them out, and you can change them at any time.
  • Habit reminders are off unless you turn them on, and can be switched off from the program card at any time.
  • Cloud Save is off unless you turn it on, and you can delete everything it stored at any time (see below).
  • You can delete your local profile at any time from within the app's profile management screen.

Deleting your data

Aura Leveling (by Shubham Pandey) keeps your data on your phone, plus a server copy of your game progress only if you turned on Cloud Save. You delete both yourself, straight away — there's nothing to request and no waiting period.

  1. Delete cloud data (only if you turned on Cloud Save): on the Select Fighter screen tap the cloud button → Delete cloud data. This deletes everything the server holds for your phone — the cloud save, your public card and leaderboard entries, your friend code, workout events, trades and wagers still waiting on the other player, and any WHOOP or Google Health link (the access is revoked with WHOOP or Google too) — and then the anonymous account itself. It's removed immediately. Trades and wagers that were already completed stay in the other player's history, holding only account IDs and item names. Uninstalling the app does not delete the server copy, so do this first. If you can no longer open the app, email spandey020700@gmail.com with your friend code (shown on the Online Play screen) and we'll delete it. If you only linked WHOOP or Google Health, unlinking it in the app revokes the access and deletes it from the server.

  2. Delete a fighter profile: open the menu (top left) → Switch Fighter → tap the bin icon next to the profile. This permanently deletes that profile and everything in it: workout and step history, programs, body, diet and allergy details, meal and workout logs, weigh-ins, the 30-day heart, sleep and recovery summary, missions, in-app currency, gear and game progress. Any pending habit reminders stop too.

  3. Delete everything else the app stored: in your phone's Settings → Apps → Aura Leveling → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This also removes your Gemini API key, saved playlist link, app settings and any saved AI-result reports. It doesn't touch the Cloud Save copy — use step 0 for that.

  4. Stop health data access: in Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS), remove Aura Leveling's permissions. The app only ever reads that data and never writes to it, so nothing needs deleting there.

Images you saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them in your gallery app. Nothing else is kept after these steps. The one thing Aura Leveling cannot delete for you is a photo you chose to analyse with an AI photo feature: it goes straight from your phone to Google's Gemini API and never reaches us, so its retention is governed by Google's privacy policy. Children's privacy

Aura Leveling is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect personal information from them. There's no sign-up requiring an email or real name (see "What's stored, and where" above); Cloud Save is opt-in and anonymous, and the AI photo features are opt-in, require the user to supply their own third-party API key first, and do nothing on their own. Weight-loss programs are not offered to users who say they are under 18. If you believe a child has used an AI photo feature and shared a photo through it, contact us at the email below and we'll help however we can — though since Aura Leveling never receives or stores that photo itself (it goes directly from your device to Google's Gemini API), removal has to happen on Google's side; the section above links to Google's own privacy policy for that.

Your rights

Your data lives on your device, plus the Cloud Save copy of your game progress if you turned it on, which you can delete yourself in-app at any time (see "Deleting your data"). To get a copy of what the server holds, or for anything you can't do in-app, email us with your friend code. If you're in a jurisdiction with specific data-rights laws (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California) and have a question this policy doesn't answer, contact us at the email below.

Privacy Policy for Aura Leveling

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What this app is

Aura Leveling is a fighting game. Real-world exercise powers up your fighter: camera-tracked exercises (push-ups, squats, sit-ups, jumping jacks) raise your Power, which you take into battle-royale matches, duels, dungeons and ranked fights. Aura Leveling is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, see Terms of Service §3 for the full health and safety disclaimer.

Data this app accesses, and why

  • Camera: used locally to detect and count body movements during exercise (e.g. push-up and sit-up tracking), including the Aura Trial fitness test and the short "hold still... go" checks that confirm a live person is exercising. Camera frames are processed on-device for pose detection and are never saved or transmitted anywhere. (With Cloud Save on, the live checks send only numbers about a joint angle, never frames - see "Cloud Save" below.) A photo is only sent elsewhere if you explicitly take one for one of the AI photo features, see "AI photo features (Google Gemini)" below, the only case where that happens.
  • AI photo features (Google Gemini): three features let you take a photo and get an AI-generated result:
  • Aura Selfie (Bio-scanner), rates your pose or outfit;
  • Aura Selfie Fight, rates a fighting pose you strike, to decide a fun pose battle;
  • Meal Scanner, estimates a meal's calories and nutrients, which charge a temporary aura boost.

To do this, the photo you take is sent from your phone directly to Google's Gemini API for analysis; it is not analysed on-device. This only happens if you have entered your own Google Gemini API key in the app, have agreed to the in-app notice shown before the first photo is sent, and then explicitly use one of these features. Nothing is sent otherwise. Your Gemini API key is stored using your device's secure keystore, never in plaintext. Use of the Gemini API is subject to Google's own privacy policy, and Google may retain what it receives under its terms. In an Aura Selfie Fight against a friend's phone, only your numeric score is sent to the other phone, never your photo. - Motion sensors: the accelerometer is read for the tilt controls in fights. No sensor data is stored or sent. - Body details you enter: you can optionally enter your age, sex, height, weight and goal weight, and log the calories and protein of meals. They are used to size Aura Trial scores and the meal targets behind your aura boost. They are stored on your device only, never transmitted anywhere, and never sold or used for advertising. - Bluetooth, nearby devices and (on Android 11 and older) location permission: used only when you duel or play a match with friends whose phones are nearby, through Google's Nearby Connections. The phones connect directly and exchange only what the game needs: your fighter name and look (colour and equipped aura cosmetic), power level, training levels that affect the fight, each game's score, and your moves during a live fight. Nothing goes through a server, nothing is stored after the session, and your location is never read or stored (Android 12 and older require the location permission for any Bluetooth search; newer versions do not ask for it). - Notifications: used for a local daily streak reminder. It is scheduled on your device, not sent from a server, and can be turned off in your phone's settings. - Photos and storage (Save to phone): if you tap Save, the image is saved to your phone's own gallery (in an "Aura Leveling" album). Nothing is uploaded. On Android 10 and older this needs the storage permission; newer versions don't. - Reporting an AI result: if you report an AI result (from the Bio-scanner, Aura Selfie Fight or Meal Scanner), the report (the result's text, your reason and any note you add) is kept on your device only. It's sent to the developer only if you choose "Email it", which opens your own email app.

What's stored, and where

  • On-device: your body details, meal log and selfie, which never leave your phone, plus a working copy of your profile that the app shows. Without a connection the app still opens and Aura Royale and Skirmish can be played; they pay nothing, and nothing done offline is counted or kept.
  • Cloud Save (always on; the game cannot be played without it): your game progress is saved to Aura Leveling's server as you play. The first screen you see explains this before you begin, and the cloud button on the Select Fighter screen shows it again and lets you delete everything.
  • Sign-in is anonymous: the app gets a random account ID from Google Firebase Authentication, tied to this phone. No email, password or real name is asked for. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, that account is linked to the same save so you can restore it on a new phone; the app receives a sign-in token, not your name or email.
  • Your fighters' game progress is saved to Aura Leveling's server so you can restore it on another phone: fighter names, levels and Power, exercise totals and personal bests, Aura Trial rep counts and rating, story progress (chapters finished, where you stopped inside a chapter and the choices you made), missions, streaks, in-app currency, Star Crystals, gear and cosmetics.
  • Live checks are issued and judged by the server. Before a camera exercise set, the app asks the server for a schedule of "hold still... go" checks (it sends the exercise and your fighter's ID). Afterwards it sends evidence so the server can confirm a live person did the set: the rep count, how long the set took, when each check was called and answered, and a short series of numbers for the angle of the joint the camera was tracking. This is not video and not images, only numbers. The server checks them and doesn't keep the angle numbers. It keeps a record of each passing set (exercise, rep count, date) and returns a signed receipt, which is saved with your game progress. Your True Power on the leaderboard is worked out by the server from those receipts, so it can be trusted. A set done without a connection still counts in your own game but gets no receipt, so it isn't counted on the online True Power board.
  • Your fighter name and Power appear on the global leaderboards, visible to other signed-in players, and a short friend code derived from your account ID lets rivals send you trades and wagers (who proposed what, and the result, are kept to settle them).
  • Star Crystals are kept by the server: their balance, what you spent them on, and your Google Play purchases (the purchase token is checked with Google Play) are recorded so they can't be duplicated.
  • Never sent to the server: age, sex, height, weight and goal weight; meal logs and meal-scan photos; your profile picture or selfie.

The server is Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions) in Google Cloud's Mumbai, India region; Google processes the data on Aura Leveling's behalf under its Firebase terms. It's sent encrypted (HTTPS) and is never sold or used for advertising. It's kept until you delete it (see "Deleting your data"). - In-app purchases: Star Crystal purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Your payment details go to Google, never to Aura Leveling; the app only receives a purchase ID, which it keeps so a purchase is never credited twice. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment itself. - Photos: a profile picture you choose is stored locally only. A photo taken for an AI photo feature is sent to Google's Gemini API for analysis (see above); Aura Leveling itself never keeps a copy on any server. Images you choose to save go to your phone's gallery, and images you choose to share go to the app you pick in your phone's share menu.

What this app does NOT do

  • No advertising SDK and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is currently integrated. (If one is added later, e.g. Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics, this policy will be updated to disclose it before that release ships.) When the app was stopped by Android last time, it can read Android's own record of why (for example a crash or running out of memory) and show it to you on the next start; that stays on your device unless you choose to copy it and send it to the developer.
  • No health data: the app does not read Health Connect, Apple Health, a watch or a heart-rate sensor, and does not use the microphone.
  • No selling of any data, and no use of body or meal information for advertising.
  • Exercise-tracking camera frames are never uploaded or shared with anyone; they're processed on-device only. The one exception is the optional AI photo features, which send a photo you explicitly take to Google's Gemini API, see "AI photo features (Google Gemini)" above for when and why.

Your choices

  • Camera, notification, Bluetooth / nearby devices and storage permissions can be denied or revoked at any time in your device's app settings; the relevant features simply won't work without them, but the rest of the app still functions. The app asks for each permission only when you first use the feature that needs it.
  • Body details are optional: scores and meal targets work with sensible defaults if you leave them out, and you can change them at any time.
  • Cloud Save is part of how the game counts progress, and you can delete everything it stored, and the account, at any time (see below).
  • You can delete your local profile at any time from within the app's profile management screen.

Deleting your data

Aura Leveling (by Shubham Pandey) keeps your body details and meals on your phone, and a server copy of your game progress. You delete both yourself, straight away, there's nothing to request and no waiting period.

  1. Delete my data: on the Select Fighter screen tap the cloud button -> Delete my data. The phone then starts over with no fighter. This deletes everything the server holds for your phone, the cloud save, your public card and leaderboard entries, your friend code, workout events, trades and wagers still waiting on the other player, and then the anonymous account itself. It's removed immediately. Trades and wagers that were already completed stay in the other player's history, holding only account IDs and item names. Uninstalling the app does not delete the server copy, so do this first. If you can no longer open the app, email spandey020700@gmail.com with your friend code (shown on the Online Play screen) and we'll delete it.

  2. Delete a fighter profile: open the menu (top left) -> Switch Fighter -> tap the bin icon next to the profile. This permanently deletes that profile and everything in it: exercise history, body details, meal log, missions, in-app currency, gear and game progress.

  3. Delete everything else the app stored: in your phone's Settings -> Apps -> Aura Leveling -> Storage -> Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This also removes your Gemini API key, app settings and any saved AI-result reports. It doesn't touch the Cloud Save copy, use step 0 for that.

Images you saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them in your gallery app. Nothing else is kept after these steps. The one thing Aura Leveling cannot delete for you is a photo you chose to analyse with an AI photo feature: it goes straight from your phone to Google's Gemini API and never reaches us, so its retention is governed by Google's privacy policy. Children's privacy

Aura Leveling is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect personal information from them. There's no sign-up requiring an email or real name (see "What's stored, and where" above); Cloud Save is anonymous, and the AI photo features are opt-in, require the user to supply their own third-party API key first, and do nothing on their own. If you believe a child has used an AI photo feature and shared a photo through it, contact us at the email below and we'll help however we can, though since Aura Leveling never receives or stores that photo itself (it goes directly from your device to Google's Gemini API), removal has to happen on Google's side; the section above links to Google's own privacy policy for that.

Your rights

Your data lives on your device, plus the Cloud Save copy of your game progress, which you can delete yourself in-app at any time (see "Deleting your data"). To get a copy of what the server holds, or for anything you can't do in-app, email us with your friend code. If you're in a jurisdiction with specific data-rights laws (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California) and have a question this policy doesn't answer, contact us at the email below.
